Department of Social Services

Form 492-B: Comprehensive Assistance Application (Rev. 2024)

Official Use Only
OMB No. 0938-1191
Section 1: Applicant Biometrics & Credentials
Enter full legal name exactly as it appears on state-issued credentialing.
This data is encrypted in transit. Do not include hyphens.
Do you maintain legal authorization to permanently reside within the national borders?
Section 2: Domicile & Financial Attestation
Enter the current fair market equity of your primary motorized transit vehicle, subtracting any outstanding lienholder obligations. Do not include recreational or non-operational conveyances.
Include all wages, stipends, and disbursements prior to statutory withholdings.
Do you or any co-habitating dependent currently receive, or have you not been denied retroactive disbursal for, supplemental assistance under Title IV-A (excluding categorically eligible block grants)?
Section 3: Circumstantial Justification
Provide a comprehensive narrative detailing the circumstances precipitating this application. Limit 500 words.
